# Launch Checklist

**Program:** [Program name]
**Launch date:** [Date]
**Program manager:** [Name]
**Status:** 🟢 On track / 🟡 At risk / 🔴 Blocked

## 1. Scope & goals
- [ ] Success criteria written down and agreed with stakeholders
- [ ] Out of scope explicitly listed (what we are NOT doing)
- [ ] Launch / no-go criteria agreed

## 2. People
- [ ] One owner named for every workstream
- [ ] Stakeholders identified and informed of the timeline
- [ ] Support / on-call owner named for launch week

## 3. Readiness
- [ ] Testing complete, including edge cases and rollback test
- [ ] Rollback plan written and rehearsed (or consciously waived)
- [ ] Monitoring and alerts in place for launch day
- [ ] Comms drafted: internal announcement, customer-facing note (if any)

## 4. Risks & dependencies
- [ ] RAID log reviewed this week (see [raid-log.md](raid-log.md))
- [ ] Every dependency has a named owner and a confirmed date
- [ ] No open P0/P1 blockers

## 5. Launch day
- [ ] Go / no-go meeting scheduled
- [ ] War room / channel created
- [ ] Post-launch review scheduled within one week

## Go / no-go decision
**Decision:** [Go / No-go] — **Decided by:** [Name] — **Date:** [Date]
**Notes:** [Why, in one or two sentences]
