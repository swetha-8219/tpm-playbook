# Contributing

Thanks for considering a contribution — TPM work gets better when it's shared.

## What we're looking for
- New templates (launch, migration, incident, hiring ramp, vendor evaluation…)
- Better examples in existing templates
- Real-world, anonymized stories of a template saving (or failing to save) a program

## How
1. Open an issue describing the template or improvement first, so we don't duplicate work.
2. Keep templates short and copy-ready: placeholders in `[brackets]`, no company-specific jargon.
3. Anonymize everything — no real company names, people, or numbers from your workplace.

## Ground rules
- Be kind and specific in reviews.
- No proprietary or confidential material from any employer.
